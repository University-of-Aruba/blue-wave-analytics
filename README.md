# Blue Wave Analytics: Introduction to R

The Curaçao edition of [Introduction to R for SPSS Users][master], a DCDC
Network training. Same seven episodes, same objectives, localised to Curaçao and
taught on the squad that took the island to the World Cup.

A hands-on workshop that builds on what you already know from SPSS to get you
productive in R. No programming experience required.

Built with [The Carpentries Workbench][workbench] and developed by the
[DCDC Network](https://www.nwo.nl/en/projects/wcnjy25550) at the
[University of Aruba](https://www.ua.aw/), delivered in Curaçao with the
University of Curaçao.

> **All data here is public.** The squad dataset is rebuilt from Wikipedia
> national-team squad tables by a committed script, so anyone can regenerate and
> reuse it. An earlier iteration used Cornerstone Economics research data; that
> was removed on 23 August 2026, and the reasoning is in
> [DATA-PROVENANCE.md](DATA-PROVENANCE.md).

## How this edition relates to the master course

The master product is **Introduction to R for SPSS Users**, which lives at
[University-of-Aruba/r-for-spss-users][master-repo] and carries Zenodo concept
DOI [10.5281/zenodo.20057762](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20057762). Islands
are editions underneath it, not rebrands on top of it. The sequence, objectives,
and teaching beats are identical across editions; only the data and the
narrative are local.

Each edition is deposited as a new version under the same concept DOI, so the
lineage stays intact.

## The teaching data

Everything learners compute on is generated by
[`scripts/00_build_teaching_data.R`](scripts/00_build_teaching_data.R) and
committed to `episodes/data/`. Nothing is scraped live in the room.

| File | What it is | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| `blue_wave_squad.csv` | 94 players across four ABC island national squads | Episodes 2, 3, 4, 6 |
| `blue_wave_squad.xlsx` | Same data, sheets `curacao` and `aruba` | Episode 2 |
| `blue_wave_squad.sav` | Same data as SPSS, for the `haven` demonstration | Episode 2 |
| `fifa_rankings.csv` | FIFA rank, points, population, diaspora for 211 associations | Episodes 4, 5 |
| `diaspora_change.csv` | Diaspora stock in 1990, 2010, 2024 | Episode 5 |
| `countries_backup.csv` | Offline fallback for the Episode 1 live pull | Episode 1 |

The squad dataset is scraped from the current-squad tables of four English
Wikipedia articles. Coding decisions, variable definitions, and limitations are
documented in
[`episodes/data/blue_wave_squad_codebook.md`](episodes/data/blue_wave_squad_codebook.md).

It has the gaps real data has: two players cannot be placed at a club at all, and
the squads are only as current as Wikipedia. That is deliberate. Episodes 3, 5,
and 6 each stop to name what is being excluded and what it costs.

Re-running `scripts/00_build_teaching_data.R` will produce different numbers as
squads change, and the episode prose quotes specific figures, so re-read the
episodes after you re-run it.

**Before delivery**, read
[`episodes/data/VERIFY-BEFORE-DELIVERY.md`](episodes/data/VERIFY-BEFORE-DELIVERY.md).
One file in the folder is an empty template, not a dataset.

## Course overview

| | Day 1 | Day 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **Focus** | Why R, getting started, data manipulation, visualization | Statistical analysis, reproducible reporting, next steps |
| **Duration** | 5 to 6 hours with breaks | 5 to 6 hours with breaks |
| **Approach** | Every SPSS operation paired with its R equivalent | Hands-on exercises on Dutch Caribbean data |

## Episodes

1. **The Case for Switching**, live demo of what R can do that SPSS cannot
2. **Your First R Session**, RStudio orientation, importing CSV, Excel and `.sav`, descriptive statistics
3. **Data Manipulation**, filtering, sorting, recoding with tidyverse, mapped to SPSS menus
4. **Visualization with ggplot2**, publication-quality charts
5. **Statistical Analysis**, t-tests, correlation, chi-square, regression
6. **Reproducible Reporting**, R Markdown for combining analysis and write-up
7. **Where to Go from Here**, resources, Dutch Caribbean data packages, community

## The capstone

[`episodes/files/blue-wave-squad-report-template.Rmd`](episodes/files/blue-wave-squad-report-template.Rmd)
is a parameterised squad report. Change `params$team` from `CUW-M` to `ARU-W`,
knit, and you get a different report from the same file. Episode 1 shows the
finished output as the target; Episode 6 hands learners the source.

## Target audience

Researchers, students, lecturers, and institutional staff who currently use SPSS
and want to move to a free, open-source alternative. No programming experience
required; basic statistical knowledge is enough.
